Conflict

One of the primary reasons for world hunger is conflict since it ruins all the structures in which people relied on in order to meet their basic needs. Furthermore, the World Food Programme shows data that mentions "70% of acutely food-insecure people live in fragile or conflict-affected countries"(World Food Programme), and other data that says "25% increase in conflict in 2024 compared to 2023"(World Food Programme). This data shows the correlation between conflict and world hunger.
