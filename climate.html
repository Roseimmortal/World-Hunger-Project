Climate Change

Climate change is one of the major causes of world hunger because it affects the weather and destroys the food people depend on. The World Food Programme says that “Climate extremes are a key driver of global hunger” (World Food Programme). This means things like droughts, floods, and heat waves can ruin crops and make it hard for farmers to grow enough food. When this happens, food becomes more expensive and harder for families to get. The WFP also says that “The climate crisis doesn’t have to be a hunger crisis” (World Food Programme), which shows that even though climate change is a big problem, it can be managed with the right action. Overall, climate change makes hunger worse around the world because it damages farming and reduces the amount of food available.
