Poverty

Poverty is one of the leading reasons for hunger in the world as it decreases chances of accessing food. When talking on a global scale, the production of food is decent in many areas, yet people who suffer from poverty lack access to food because of low incomes and the high price of food products. In reality those people must use all of their income to survive, which leaves no money left for eating nutritious food or paying medical expenses, among other costs. Research states that millions of people across the globe are unable to afford a healthy lifestyle because of their limited financial resources. Data provide by "Our World in Data", emphasizes that 70% of the population in 7 countries in Africa face extreme poverty.
